CONFIDENCE-GATED ROUTING
Evaluates Output based on Mean Confidence & Std Deviation
TIER 1 (SAFE)
>90% Conf, <5% Uncert.
Auto-approve. Normal DICOM SR generated.
TIER 2 (WARN)
70-90% Conf OR 5-10% Uncert.
Flags DICOM SR for review.
TIER 3 (URGENT)
<70% Conf OR >10% Uncert.
Urgent review, pushes to Active Learning.
